## Overview

Route: London, Edinburgh, Glasgow, Belfast, Dublin.

An eight-day city-hopping route across the UK and Ireland, beginning with a direct flight from Delhi to London. The longest travel legs happen overnight or in the morning, leaving the day free for sightseeing.

---

## Day 1, Delhi to London

Fly from Delhi to London. Keep the first evening light after arrival and settle into the city.

## Day 2, London

Walking day through central London. This route is best done at an easy pace, with time to stop for food and photos.

- Trafalgar Square
- The London Eye
- Big Ben
- Westminster Abbey
- Buckingham Palace
- Hyde Park

## Day 3, London

Free day in London. Take an overnight bus to Edinburgh.

## Day 4, Edinburgh to Glasgow

Arrive in Edinburgh early morning and explore before continuing to Glasgow in the evening.

- Edinburgh Castle
- Royal Mile
- Whisky experience
- Dean Village
- Arthur's Seat
- Calton Hill

## Day 5, Glasgow to Belfast

Travel from Glasgow to Belfast in the morning. Keep the rest of the day open to settle in and explore Belfast at a slower pace.

## Day 6, Belfast to Dublin

Start in Belfast, then take the roughly two-hour journey to Dublin.

- Titanic Belfast
- Carrickfergus Castle *(optional)*

## Day 7, Dublin

Walking day through central Dublin.

- St. Stephen's Green
- Temple Bar
- Ha'penny Bridge
- Dublin Castle
- St. Patrick's Cathedral

## Day 8, Dublin to Delhi

Head to the airport for the return flight.
