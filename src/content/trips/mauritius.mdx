## Overview

Route: La Gaulette, Grand Baie, east coast day trips, airport belt.

A self-drive loop around the island, anchored on two apartment stays and a final night near the airport. Fly into Sir Seewoosagur Ramgoolam International Airport (MRU), pick up a rental car, and work clockwise: southwest first for the volcanic interior and Chamarel, then north for the islet tours, then east for the lava tubes and lagoon stops before checking into an airport hotel for an early departure.

May through December is the drier window on the west and north coasts, which lines up well with this route.

### Getting around

A rental car for the full trip is the move. Pick it up at MRU after you land and freshen up at the airport; we left around 10 AM once the car was ready. Drop it at the airport on departure morning. The main roads are fine for a compact car; island-hopping days are organised tours that handle the boat legs.

### Money

We took US dollars from India and exchanged at the airport on arrival. EUR is also widely used on the island if you would rather carry euros.

### SIM and data

A local SIM at MRU on arrival is the easy option if you want data for maps and tour confirmations on the road.

### Vegetarian food

Vegetarian food is straightforward across the island. Indian restaurants are common, and most places will do veg curries, roti, and rice plates if you ask.

## Day 1, Arrival and the southwest

Land at MRU and freshen up at the airport before the rental pickup. We left around 10 AM once the car was ready.

Breakfast on the drive south, then **Grand Bassin** (Ganga Talao), the sacred crater lake and one of the island's headline Hindu pilgrimage sites. **Black Gorges National Park** filled the rest of the afternoon, a good first taste of the interior forest and viewpoints. Check in at La Gaulette for the southwest stretch.

## Day 2, Chamarel

Day trip to **Chamarel**: the Seven Coloured Earths, the nearby waterfall, and the rum distilleries in the area. Easy to cover from La Gaulette without rushing.

## Day 3, Northbound via Pamplemousses

Checkout from La Gaulette and drive north. **Sir Seewoosagur Ramgoolam Botanical Garden** (Pamplemousses) and **L'Aventure du Sucre**, the sugar museum, are natural stops on the way to the north coast. Check in at Grand Baie.

## Day 4, Northern islets

Pre-booked day tour to the northern islets: **Coin de Mire**, **Flat Island**, and **Îlot Gabriel**. Snorkelling and beach time on the boat circuit; book ahead in peak season.

## Day 5, East coast and lava tubes

Checkout from Grand Baie and head east. **Roche Noire lava tubes** first, then the coastal stops around **Mahébourg**, **Pointe d'Esny**, and **Blue Bay** for lagoon views and beach time. Check in at the Holiday Inn near the airport for the early flight out.

## Day 6, Five-island tour

Full-day **five-island tour** covering **Ile aux Aigrettes** and **Ile aux Cerfs**, with boat transfers and beach stops on the east side. Another pre-book day.

## Day 7, Departure

Early start to MRU, drop the rental car, and fly home.
