## Overview

Route: Kathmandu, Pokhara, Kathmandu.

A short Kathmandu and Pokhara split: land in Kathmandu, ease in with Thamel and the old-city sights, then fly across to Pokhara for the lakeside stretch and the sunrise outing to Sarangkot, before circling back to Kathmandu for the temples and the flight home.

Nepal's broad sweet spots are **spring (March to May)** and **autumn (September to November)**, when skies are clearest and mountain views are most reliable. This trip was in January, which still worked fine for a city-and-Pokhara route, just colder in the mornings and evenings.

### Flights

The Kathmandu to Pokhara flight is the time-saver on this route. It is a short domestic hop, around 20 minutes in the air, and cuts out the long road transfer.

### SIM and money

The local currency is the **Nepalese rupee (NPR)**.

**INR 100 notes** were accepted at many places. I exchanged some Nepalese rupees at shops once I got in, and kept cash for the smaller spends.

I took a SIM card from a local shop, but if I did it again I would just get it at the airport and be sorted the moment I land.

## Day 1, Arrival in Kathmandu

Fly **DEL to KTM**, check in, then keep the first day easy.

- Explore **Thamel**
- Dinner and relax

## Day 2, Kathmandu sightseeing

Classic Kathmandu city day.

- **Patan Durbar Square**
- **Swayambhunath** (Monkey Temple)
- More Kathmandu sightseeing around the old city

## Day 3, Pokhara

Take the **11 AM flight to Pokhara**, check in, and sort the next day's tour.

- Explore the market
- Lakeside walks
- Sunset at **Phewa Lake**

## Day 4, Pokhara sightseeing

Early start for **Sarangkot** for sunrise, then a full-day Pokhara sightseeing tour by bus.

- **Gupteshwar Cave**
- **Devi's Fall**
- **Pumdikot Shiva Statue**
- **World Peace Pagoda**
- **Hanging Bridge**
- **Bat Cave**
- **Mahendra Cave**
- Temple stop on the tour

## Day 5, Kathmandu

Fly or return back to Kathmandu and use the day for the other big temple-and-stupa circuit.

- **Pashupatinath**
- **Boudhanath Stupa**
- **Kathmandu Durbar Square**

## Day 6, Travel out

Early morning flight **KTM to DEL**.
