## Overview

Route: Kathmandu, Pokhara, Kathmandu.

A short Kathmandu and Pokhara split: land in Kathmandu, ease in with Thamel and the old-city sights, then fly across to Pokhara for the lakeside stretch and the sunrise outing to Sarangkot, before circling back to Kathmandu for the temples and the flight home.

Nepal's broad sweet spots are **spring (March to May)** and **autumn (September to November)**, when skies are clearest and mountain views are most reliable. This trip was in January, which still worked fine for a city-and-Pokhara route, just colder in the mornings and evenings.

### Getting around

The Kathmandu to Pokhara leg is easiest by **domestic flight**. It is a short hop, around 20 minutes in the air, and saves you the much longer road transfer, so it is worth it on a short trip like this.

Within the cities, you can get taxis easily, but on this route a lot of the practical things are close by once you are in the main areas.

### SIM and money

The local currency is the **Nepalese rupee (NPR)**.

**INR 100 notes** were accepted at many places. I exchanged some Nepalese rupees at shops once I got in, and kept cash for the smaller spends.

Either get an **eSIM** before you fly, or pick up the SIM at the airport itself and get the **Pathao** app set up so you can book a cab from the airport.

Otherwise you are stuck either booking the prepaid airport taxi, which is more expensive, or bargaining on the road after you step out.

### Veg food

Vegetarian food is widely available on this route. **Dal bhat**, vegetable curries, **chiya**, and **veg momos** are all easy finds in Kathmandu and Pokhara, so you do not need to overplan meals.

## Day 1, Arrival in Kathmandu

Fly **Delhi to Kathmandu**, check in, then keep the first day easy.

- Explore **Thamel** on foot, the easiest part of Kathmandu to settle into on arrival, with cafes, gear shops, money changers, bars, and tour counters all packed in close
- Book or confirm the next day's city plan
- Dinner and relax after the flight

If you land with some daylight left, just use it for a slow wander and keep the radius tight instead of trying to cram in monuments straight away.

## Day 2, Kathmandu sightseeing

Classic Kathmandu heritage day.

- **Patan Durbar Square**, one of the three royal squares of the valley and part of the wider **Kathmandu Valley UNESCO World Heritage Site**, for carved Newar architecture, temple courtyards, and old palace buildings
- **Swayambhunath** (Monkey Temple), the hilltop stupa west of the city, one of Nepal's most important Buddhist sites and another UNESCO-listed stop, with wide views over the valley
- More Kathmandu sightseeing around the old city

The monkey nickname at Swayambhunath is real, so keep your food and sunglasses in hand.

## Day 3, Pokhara

Take the **morning flight to Pokhara**, check in, and sort the next day's tour.

- Explore the market
- Lakeside walks around the calmer half of the trip, with **Phewa Lake** and the Annapurna range shaping the whole city
- Sunset at **Phewa Lake**

Keep this day light. The whole point is to settle into the lakeside area, walk without an agenda, and save your energy for the early start next morning. Sunset by the lake is the obvious first-evening move, especially if the sky is clear enough for the mountain outline to show.

## Day 4, Pokhara sightseeing

Early start for **Sarangkot** for sunrise, then a full-day Pokhara sightseeing tour by bus.

- **Sarangkot** for sunrise, the big viewpoint above Pokhara, around **1,600 metres** up, with wide Himalayan views toward **Annapurna, Dhaulagiri, and Manaslu** on a clear morning
- **Gupteshwar Cave** and **Devi's Fall**, the best-known pair on the city circuit, sitting opposite each other, with the water from Devi's Fall passing through the cave system
- **Pumdikot Shiva Statue**
- **World Peace Pagoda**, one of the big elevated viewpoints over the lake and city
- **Hanging Bridge**
- **Bat Cave**
- **Mahendra Cave and temple**

This is usually a packed vehicle day rather than a slow wandering day, good for covering the postcard sights in one shot.

## Day 5, Kathmandu

Fly or return back to Kathmandu and use the day for the other big temple-and-stupa circuit.

- **Pashupatinath**, one of the most important Shiva temples in South Asia, spread across a large sacred precinct on the **Bagmati River** and around **25 minutes on foot from our stay near the airport**
- **Boudhanath Stupa**, one of the largest spherical stupas in Nepal and the centre of the Tibetan Buddhist quarter, best experienced by walking the clockwise kora around it
- **Kathmandu Durbar Square**, the old royal centre at Hanuman Dhoka, another UNESCO-listed square with palace buildings and layered Newar temple architecture

If you still have energy in the evening, Boudha is the nicest place to linger after dark; Kathmandu Durbar is more of a daytime heritage stop.

## Day 6, Travel out

Early morning flight **Kathmandu to Delhi**.
